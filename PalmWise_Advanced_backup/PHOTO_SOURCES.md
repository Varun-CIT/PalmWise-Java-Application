# PalmWise Advanced Photo Sources

The UI uses real agricultural/support photographs from the following public sources.

- PM-KISAN / farmer tractor: Wikimedia Commons, `Farmer working in the field with their tractor.jpg` (CC0/public-domain dedication as listed on Commons).
- Rice Support Scheme: Wikimedia Commons, `FARMERS ENGAGED IN RICE CULTIVATION, KUTTANAD.jpg` (CC BY-SA 4.0).
- Wheat Support Scheme: Wikimedia Commons, `Wheat Field in India.jpg`.
- Cereal Productivity Support: Wikimedia Commons, `Finger Millet Field at Peddamunagalachedu Village.jpg` (CC BY-SA 4.0).
- Cereals & Pulses: Wikimedia Commons, pearl-millet/bajra photograph.
- Tree Plantation & Agroforestry: Wikimedia Commons, `Teak Plantation.jpg` (CC BY-SA 4.0).
- Coconut: Wikimedia Commons, `Cocos nucifera plantation in continental India (Nagesh).jpg` (CC BY-SA 2.0).
- Oil Palm: Wikimedia Commons, `Oilpalm Mizoram DSC7011.jpg` (CC BY-SA 4.0).
- Cashew/Nut crops: Wikimedia Commons, `Cashew Plantations in Dodamarg, Sindhudurg, Maharashtra, India.jpg` (CC0).
- Cotton: Wikimedia Commons, `Cottonfieldindia.jpg` (CC BY-SA 4.0).
- Turmeric: Wikimedia Commons, `Field of Turmeric with coconut as a inter crop` (CC BY-SA 3.0).
- Assistance Centre: Pexels, `Man and woman wearing a headset with a microphone in an office`.

Image URLs are used remotely by the React frontend; the browser therefore needs internet access to display them.
